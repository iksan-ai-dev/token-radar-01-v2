What this page claims, and how you can check it

Every ranked protocol records no token in DefiLlama's data: no CoinGecko id and no symbol. For each one the model states an implied value — what its token would be worth if it traded like the — tokened protocols in the same snapshot, at the same TVL. The number is not a forecast and not a promise of a launch. It is a comparable-valuation statement, and the page recomputes it in front of you: every row's drawer shows TVL × curve multiple × category adjustment × shrunk tilts = implied value, with each factor printed to four decimals.

The board covers the whole watchlist first and then every further pre-token protocol under the floor — — rows, one rule applied to all of them, sorted so rank 1 holds the largest implied value. Nothing is hand-picked, and no name is hardcoded.

The model, in numbers a reader can audit

Fitted, then shrunk

TermCoefficienttWeight used

Weights are empirical-Bayes shrunk: coefficient × t²/(t²+1) × 0.5 — small-t terms are kept only in proportion to the fit's confidence, then halved, so they break near-ties without overruling the comparables.

Out of sample, honestly

Leave-one-out: refit without one comparable, predict it, take the fold error, median across —. Judged against the flat median it replaces.

What was rejected

An unshrunk growth multiplier at weight 0.5 cost rank correlation and inflated out-of-sample fold error, so growth, age and chain count enter only as the shrunk tilt above (their own t-statistics miss 1.98). A full-weight category adjustment overfits thin categories, so it is held at —.

Multiplier = 10^(shrunk median residual × weight). A category with no tokened comparable is absent here and multiplies by 1.000.

What counts as a pre-token candidate

Included

A protocol whose DefiLlama record carries no gecko id and no symbol, TVL above zero, that is not itself a venue or wrapper. Bridges, CEXs, liquid staking and restaking are out: their TVL is other protocols' deposits and would double-count, as are version tails (… V3) and wrapped or staked names that mirror an existing token.

The watchlist is the floor of the board, never its ceiling: — watchlist protocols are covered, then every further candidate under the $— floor follows on the same rule.

Set aside, and why that is the honest call

— protocols already record a token; they are the model's comparables, not its output. — pre-token protocols were excluded by the venue or wrapper rule above. Sitting in the snapshot but not on the board is a decision, not an oversight, and the rule that makes it is printed here rather than left implicit.

What this page does not claim

Market cap is circulating, not fully diluted: a new token's FDV usually runs 2–5× its circulating cap, so these values are conservative on that axis. No launch, date or airdrop is promised; farm steps say "may qualify", never "will receive".
