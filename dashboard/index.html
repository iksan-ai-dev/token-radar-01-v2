Methodology — the value model, in full

Fitted size curve, not a flat median. The snapshot's own 128 tokened comparables show that market-cap-per-TVL falls as protocols get bigger. We fit, at runtime, log10(mcap/TVL) = a + b·log10(TVL) — elasticity −0.48, so the multiple is about 0.48× at $10M TVL and 0.033× at $2.76B TVL. A flat category median would overvalue the largest protocols roughly 3×; the curve is what the snapshot's own numbers say.

Category adjustment. Each category's median residual from the curve is shrunk toward zero by n/(n+4) (4 pseudo-comparables) and applied as a multiplier. Categories with no comparables get no adjustment.

Multipliers. Implied value = TVL × curve multiple × category adjustment × growth × age × breadth. Growth = 1 + 0.5·clamp(change_7d/25, −1, 1). Age: under 6 months 0.85, 6–12 months 0.95, 1–4 years rising 1.00→1.15, then fading to 0.85 — mature pre-token protocols are the most credible launches. Breadth = 1 + 0.08·ln(chains), capped 1.25. Capped at $10B, rounded to whole dollars.

No quality discount. We do not discount Risk Curators or Onchain Capital Allocators by 0.5: the size curve already penalises the giants, and the comparables rank the big curators at the top without a discount — an extra 0.5 would double-count and diverge from the data.

Uncertainty. Residual SD is — in log10, so each value carries a P25–P75 range of implied ÷ — to implied × —.

Validation (leave-one-out). Refit the curve 128 times, each time holding one comparable out: median prediction error —. A flat-median baseline on the same test errs by —. The size curve beats the naive model — that is the point.

What the model gets wrong. The comparable with the largest leave-one-out residual is — (residual — in log10): tiny, volatile tokens can trade at multiples hundreds of times the curve. The model is a median story, not a promise for any one name.

What we do not claim. mcap here is circulating market cap, not FDV — a new token's FDV is usually 2–5× its circulating mcap, so these values are conservative on that axis. We do not predict whether or when any token launches, and we never promise an airdrop. Farm steps say “may qualify” / “positions for”, never “will receive”.
